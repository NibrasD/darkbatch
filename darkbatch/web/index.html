DarkBatch: Technical Whitepaper & Architectural Summary

DarkBatch brings the venture-backed market structure innovation of Archer Exchange (4th Place DeFi in Colosseum Cypherpunk) directly to Zcash NU6.3 (Ironwood Era).

1. Why Frequent Batch Auctions (FBA)?

Continuous order books suffer from latency wars and MEV (Miner Extractable Value) where front-running bots steal spread from institutional traders. DarkBatch aggregates limit intents over discrete 30-second epochs. Arrival order is irrelevant. The solver determines a single Uniform Clearing Price P* where aggregate demand meets supply.

2. ZIP-228 Shielded Swaps & Action Groups

Each Swap Order (SO) forms an ActionGroup. The binding keys are summed up ($bsk_{\text{bundle}} = \sum bsk_i \pmod q$), proving that the bundle conserves value on-chain without disclosing individual amounts or counterparties.

3. Ironwood Post-Orchard Compatibility

Settlement transactions conform to ZIP 229 (Transaction v6) targeting the Ironwood shielded pool, with 512-byte ChaCha20-Poly1305 encrypted execution receipts.
